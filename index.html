    How the tiles are made

    Grid

    • Flat-top hex, 32 px wide and 28 px tall, side 16.
    • Columns step 24 px, odd columns sit 14 px lower (Tiled: stagger X).
    • Each tile is a 32×48 cell. Trees may rise 16 px above the face, and a 3 px soil band shows on the open edges.
    • Tiles draw top to bottom, so a tree always overlaps the tile behind it.

    Bright tiles

    • One light from the upper left on every sprite and every border.
    • Each tile has a dark outer line, a light bevel on its upper-left edges and a shade line on its lower-right edges.
    • Sprites are drawn by hand and outlined selectively: darker on the bottom and right, softer on the top and left.
    • Neighbouring tiles of the same terrain alternate a mirrored layout, so a field never repeats.
    • River tiles open the edges their neighbours need, so one river runs unbroken from the peaks to the lake. Beaches flood only the edges that face water.
