Verdant Hex Tiles

A hand-drawn hex tile set built to the standard of The Clover Patch's Fantasy Hex Tiles: flat-top 32×28 hexes that tile with no gaps, colored borders, and sprites drawn pixel by pixel. The same layouts also render as a dark strategy map in the style of your screenshot.

The tile set

Fifteen terrains, from frozen peaks to desert, swamp and coast. Each card shows the bright tile on the left and the strategy-map tile on the right. Both read the same layout, so a tree on one is a canopy on the other.

    How the tiles are made

    Grid

    • Flat-top hex, 32 px wide and 28 px tall, side 16.
    • Columns step 24 px, odd columns sit 14 px lower (Tiled: stagger X).
    • Each tile is a 32×48 cell. Trees may rise 16 px above the face, and a 3 px soil band shows on the open edges.
    • Tiles draw top to bottom, so a tree always overlaps the tile behind it.

    Bright tiles

    • One light from the upper left on every sprite and every border.
    • Each tile has a dark outer line, a light bevel on its upper-left edges and a shade line on its lower-right edges.
    • Sprites are drawn by hand and outlined selectively: darker on the bottom and right, softer on the top and left.
    • Neighbouring tiles of the same terrain alternate a mirrored layout, so a field never repeats.
    • River tiles open the edges their neighbours need, so one river runs unbroken from the peaks to the lake. Beaches flood only the edges that face water.

    Strategy map

    • Seen from straight above, flat colors, with a thin dark grid between hexes.
    • Open water turns shallow blue within 3 px of land.
    • Fog of war is a cloud mass built from puffs, lit on the upper left.
    • Lime tiles with a hatch show where the selected unit can move. Forest and hills cost more, and water and fog block it.
