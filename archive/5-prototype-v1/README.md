# Prototype v1: the clean Verdant Reach

A frozen, playable copy of the first game prototype. Open `play.html` in a browser.

- **Map:** the original Verdant Reach (16×11 hexes), healthy land under fog, with the dock on the lake.
- **Play:** assign workers to tiles to chop and fish, pay upkeep in gold and food, sell logs and planks at the
  dock, fill dock contracts to lift the fog, and buy Axes, Fishing nets, Baskets, Sawmill, Forestry and Log rafting.
- **Why it is frozen:** the design moved to *Restore the Valley*: a ruined valley, no goal text, a run that ends
  when the crew dies, and land bought with gold. Version 2 gets a new ruined map, so this copy keeps its own
  `tiles.js` and saves under its own key (`hexciv-v1-save`).
