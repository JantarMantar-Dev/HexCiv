# Prototype v2: Restore the Valley, first pass

A frozen, playable copy. Open `play.html` in a browser.

The ruined Verdant Reach with one job per ruin (clear, plant, compost, burn, filter), land bought with gold, workers
who die of hunger, cold, rot or flood, and no goal text. Workers are placed on tiles one by one.

Frozen when version 3 replaced the one-step ruins with soil, food and water values per tile, tile plans, crews and
the dock's keep lines (see the Healing the Land design doc).
