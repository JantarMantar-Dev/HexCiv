# Prototype v4: Playing on the Hex

A frozen, playable copy. Open `play.html` in a browser.

Camps placed on the map that house a trade (dock, woodcutter, gardener, jetty, well, seed bed, fire watch) and grow
in three levels as the land heals, an action bar for the selected hex, tapping a hex to lend a hand, gold from living
land, finds and eurekas, clickable locks into the tech tree, and Send to Claude debug reports.

Frozen when version 5 removed trades and began to unfold the valley in chapters (see the "Making the Valley Unfold"
design doc): play showed that hidden chains and trade deadlocks left the player asking what to do next.
