# Prototype v3: Healing the Land

A frozen, playable copy. Open `play.html` in a browser.

Soil, food and water on every land tile, healing in season-gated steps, tile plans (Forest, Field, Rest) chosen in
a side panel, four global crews (land, water, wood, food) that pick their own tasks, the dock's keep lines and the
trader's boat, a six-branch tech tree, lenses and a season ribbon.

Frozen when version 4 moved play back onto the hexes: camps placed on the map that grow as the land heals, an action
ring on each hex, tapping to help, and gold from living land (see the "Playing on the Hex" design doc).
