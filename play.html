    Regrow the Valley

    Regrow the valley

    The settlers left the Verdant Reach dead: dust, stumps, a rotting river and a drained marsh. You have a handful of leaves. Spend them on restorers; every hex a restorer heals pays leaves back.

    1. Wind pump loosens dust and clears dead trees around it, and powers sprinklers.
    2. Sprinkler turns the bare ground around it to grass. It needs a pump within 2 hexes.
    3. Tree planter turns the grass around it to forest. It needs water within 2 hexes.
    4. Scrubber, reed filter, water gate deal with the rot, the river and the marsh. The old dam can be opened once.

    Pick a restorer, then point at a hex: the hexes it would change light up, with the leaves they pay. Tap (or tap twice on a phone) to place it. Undo is free.

    Meet the four goals, then recycle every restorer so the valley looks as if no one had been there.

    The valley is alive

    Birds over the forests, fish in the river, water in the marsh, and no trace of you.
